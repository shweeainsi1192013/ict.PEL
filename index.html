Every Child Deserves an Education

Why Does Education Matter?

Education gives children the knowledge and skills they need to understand the world and prepare for their future. It helps them develop communication, problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.Education can also create better opportunities, improve communities and help people build a better future.

Education as a Right

  • Every child has the right to receive an education.
  • Education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background
  • Every child should have an equal opportunity to learn and develop their future.

Education in Bangladesh

Bangladesh has made progress in getting more children into school. However, many students still face difficulties in receiving a high-quality education.
Problems such as limited resources, school closures, unequal opportunities and different education systems can affect students' learning

Why Does This Happen in Bangladesh?

Education inequality in Bangladesh happens because many children face barriers that make it difficult to attend school regularly. Poverty is one major reason, as some families cannot afford school costs and may need their children to work or help at home. Natural disasters, especially floods and cyclones, can damage schools, roads, and learning materials, making it difficult for students in affected areas to continue their education. Children living in remote or rural areas may also have fewer schools and have to travel long distances to attend classes. In addition, some schools have limited facilities, teachers, books, and technology, which can affect the quality of education students receive. These challenges can cause children to miss school, fall behind in their studies, or leave education completely. Therefore, although Bangladesh has made progress in education, some children still face serious barriers to getting the education they deserve.

Our Aim

To understand why some children are left behind and explore ways to make education accessible to everyone.
